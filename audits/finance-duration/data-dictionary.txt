All currency columns are GBP. All data are hypothetical.
scenario_id: Pricing-case letter plus the number of equal monthly periods.
pricing_case: A or B; invented terms from scenarios.json, never a lender ID.
equal_months: Number of periods, each1/12year; not actual calendar months/days.
fully_drawn_principal_gbp: Constant outstanding principal before repayment.
assumed_nominal_annual_rate: Decimal annual simple rate, e.g.0.09 means9%.
assumed_one_off_cash_fee_rate: Decimal fee rate applied once to principal.
monthly_cash_interest_gbp: Principal multiplied by annual rate, divided by12.
separate_cash_fee_gbp: Principal multiplied by fee rate, paid once separately.
total_cash_interest_gbp: Sum of all monthly interest, excluding fees/principal.
total_finance_cost_excluding_principal_gbp: Interest plus separate fee.
principal_repaid_at_final_period_end_gbp: Principal returned at end of term;
excluded from finance costs. No intermediate principal repayments modelled.

Monthly file:
equal_month: Period number, starting at1.
starting_drawn_principal_gbp: Principal outstanding at that period start.
cash_interest_gbp: Interest for that single equal period, paid in cash.
separate_cash_fee_gbp: Full one-off fee in period1, zero in later periods.
principal_repaid_at_period_end_gbp: Full principal in final period, otherwise0.
finance_cost_excluding_principal_gbp: Period interest plus fee; no principal.
cumulative_finance_cost_excluding_principal_gbp: Running sum of period costs.

model-results.json has separately named same-horizon pricing and
delay-versus-earlier-budget comparisons.1.5equal periods is an arithmetic
coverage threshold, not45calendar days, a grace period or an extension right.
