HYPOTHETICAL cash-flow illustration, not an observed scheme, valuation, funding recommendation or market forecast. Three sales calendars with identical nominal prices and costs. Months are relative project months 0-14. Costs precede receipts within each month. Debt/equity, lender repayment and release rules, finance charges, tax/VAT and discounting are excluded. No actual lender is named.

Run python3 reproduce.py in this folder to reproduce monthly_cashflows.csv (45 rows) and scenario_summary.csv (3 rows). Scenarios must not be mistaken for the observed Q3 sales context (q3_sales_context.csv, 8 rows). The Q3 snapshot is provisional, latest transaction28August2026, September absent, registration lag affected. The hypothetical £400,000 home price is chosen independently.

All-sales-together peak £3,400,000; phased peak £2,680,000; phased receipts two months later peak £3,160,000. Each ends with £600,000 before finance/tax. Month-end-only peaks differ. Actual project cash flows need scheme-specific evidence and funding restrictions.
